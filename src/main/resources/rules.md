# Rules for each ticket option

Rotkreuz - Luzern / Luzern - Rotkreuz
## Normal tickets
- Cost up front: 0.0
- Cost per trip: 10.40 (one way), 20.80 (day pass)
- Covers: All zones between Luzern and Rotkreuz
- Catch: one way is 2 hours, while day pass is until 5 am the following day

## Half-Fare card (youth)
- Cost up front: 120.0 per year
- Cost per trip: 5.20 (one way), 10.40 (day pass)
- Covers: All zones between Luzern and Rotkreuz
- Catch: one way is about 2 hours, day pass until 5am the next day. Youth price only until 25.

## Half-Fare card (adult)
- Cost up front: 190.0 per year
- Cost per trip: 5.20 (one way), 10.40 (day pass)
- Covers: All zones between Luzern and Rotkreuz
- Catch: one way is 2 hours, while day pass is until 5 am the following day

## Passepartout monthly pass, 4 zones (youth)
- Cost up front: 119.0 per month
- Cost per trip: 0 (unlimited inside the 4 zones)
- Covers: all trains and buses in zones 10, 28, 38 (Rotkreuz - Luzern + Luzern city buses)
- Catch: Zug and Zürich not included. Youth price only until 25. Worth it from about 6 Luzern days a month.

## Passepartout monthly pass, 4 zones (adult)
- Cost up front: 163.0 per month
- Cost per trip: 0 (unlimited inside the 4 zones)
- Covers: all trains and buses in zones 10, 28, 38 (Rotkreuz - Luzern + Luzern city buses)
- Catch: Zug and Zürich not included. Worth it from about 8 Luzern days a month.

## Passepartout yearly pass, 4 zones (youth)
- Cost up front: 1071.0 per year (same as paying 9 months of the monthly pass)
- Cost per trip: 0 (unlimited inside the 4 zones, every day for 12 months)
- Covers: all trains and buses in zones 10, 28, 38 (Rotkreuz - Luzern + Luzern city buses)
- Catch: you pay the whole year at once. Zug and Zürich not included. Youth price only until 25. Only worth it if I stay and travel all 12 months (about 52+ Luzern days a year). I'm away over Christmas and leaving after summer, so probably not for me.

## Passepartout FlexiAbo 100 days, 4 zones (youth)
- Cost up front: 643.0 per year
- Cost per trip: 0 on the 100 days I choose to travel, normal tickets from day 101
- Covers: all trains and buses in zones 10, 28, 38, but only on my chosen days
- Catch: works out to CHF 6.43 per travel day if I use all 100. Pays off after about 31 Luzern days in the year. Zug and Zürich not included. Youth price only until 25.

## GA (youth)
- Cost up front: 2780.0 per year
- Cost per trip: 0 everywhere in Switzerland
- Covers: all my routes (Luzern, Zug, Zürich) plus almost all trains, buses and boats in Switzerland
- Catch: very expensive up front, about CHF 232 per month. Only worth it if I travel a lot, especially long trips. Youth price only until 25.

## Example: October
- Normal tickets: 254 (10 Luzern day passes = 208, 2 Zürich singles = 46)
- Half-Fare youth: 137 (card 10 per month + half of 254 = 127)
- Passepartout monthly youth: 165 (pass 119 + Zürich 46)
- GA youth: 231.70 (2780 / 12, covers everything)
- Cheapest: Half-Fare, saves 117 compared to what I paid

## Leaving-after-summer rule
- Count every month from now until I leave after summer 2027
- Skip the time I'm in Tanzania over Christmas (from 22 Dec)
- Yearly cards cost the full year anyway; monthly passes only for months I'm here
